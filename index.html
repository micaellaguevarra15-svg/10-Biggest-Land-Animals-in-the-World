10 Biggest Land Animals in the World

Discover the magnificent giants of the animal kingdom. Explore their sizes, habitats, and amazing facts.

Explore Animals

Meet the Giants

Explore the ten largest land animals and discover what makes them amazing.

01. African Bush Elephant

The African Bush Elephant is the largest living land animal. It is known for its enormous ears, long trunk, and tusks.

Habitat: African savannas and grasslands

Weight: Up to 6,000 kg

02. Asian Elephant

The Asian Elephant is one of the largest land animals. It has smaller ears than the African elephant and a rounded head.

Habitat: Forests and grasslands in Asia

Weight: Up to 5,000 kg

03. African Forest Elephant

The African Forest Elephant lives in the tropical forests of Central and West Africa. It has smaller, rounder ears and straighter tusks.

Habitat: Tropical rainforests in Africa

Weight: Up to 4,000 kg

04. White Rhinoceros

The White Rhinoceros is a massive plant-eating animal. It has a wide, square-shaped mouth that helps it graze on grass.

Habitat: African grasslands and savannas

Weight: Up to 2,700 kg

05. Indian Rhinoceros

The Indian Rhinoceros has thick, armor-like skin and one large horn. It is also known as the Greater One-Horned Rhinoceros.

Habitat: Grasslands and wetlands in India and Nepal

Weight: Up to 2,100 kg

06. Hippopotamus

The Hippopotamus is a large semi-aquatic mammal. It spends much of the day in water to keep its body cool.

Habitat: Rivers and lakes in sub-Saharan Africa

Weight: Up to 2,600 kg

07. Giraffe

The Giraffe is the tallest living land animal. It has a very long neck and legs that help it reach leaves high in trees.

Habitat: African savannas and woodlands

Weight: Up to 1,200 kg

08. Gaur

The Gaur is a large wild cattle species. It has a muscular body, strong legs, and curved horns.

Habitat: Forests in South and Southeast Asia

Weight: Up to 1,500 kg

09. Cape Buffalo

The Cape Buffalo is a powerful wild bovine with strong curved horns. It usually lives in herds for protection.

Habitat: African grasslands and woodlands

Weight: Up to 900 kg

10. Polar Bear

The Polar Bear is the largest living bear. It has thick fur and a layer of body fat that help it survive in freezing conditions.

Habitat: Arctic sea ice and coastal areas

Weight: Up to 800 kg
